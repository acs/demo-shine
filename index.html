How to use

DemoShine · Quick Reference ·

1
Choose what to record
When the app opens, your browser asks what to share — pick a screen, window or tab (full screen is preselected) and tick Share tab audio / Share system audio for sound — the browser decides whether that box is there and whether it starts ticked, so it is worth a look every time. The preview goes live straight away, and Reset takes you back to the picker if you forgot the audio. Use the camera button in the header to add a webcam bubble and the microphone button for your voice (both greyed out if there is no device); the mic level shows in the little meter and is mixed with the tab audio.
2
Hit record
Press the crimson button to start recording — a 3-second countdown gives you a beat, and pressing the button again during it cancels. Press again to stop. Press it once more after a recording and the new take is added to the end of the clip, as many times as you like. The share is kept between takes — so they all come from the same tab, window or screen, and there is no second picker. The timeline is there while you record, filling in with the picture and the sound as they are captured, and it becomes the trim timeline when you stop. The red box over the preview shows exactly what will be recorded: it covers the whole screen by default, drag to pick a smaller region, and click to go back to the full screen. Everything exports as WebM, a small sharp video for PRs, docs and chat.
3
Stop and trim
Press ▶ to play the clip back with sound — it plays within your IN/OUT range, and you can still drag the handles while it plays. Click anywhere on the timeline to play from that moment. Drag the blue IN handle and red OUT handle to define your clip. To cut a part out: click where it starts and click where it ends, then press the green check — or just click inside the shaded section — and it leaves the timeline, which closes up over it. The red cross leaves the section in, and the small undo arrow brings back whatever you just trimmed. Drag a mark or the shaded section to move it (it resizes if you grab it near an edge), and the clip plays under it as you drag. The estimated file size updates live.
4
Export and download
Hit Export ↓ to encode and download your clip. WebM exports stream in real time through a canvas re-encode — you can watch it happen on the timeline, and it says how much of the export is left.
5
Install as a desktop app
Chrome and Edge show an install prompt (⊕) in the address bar. Click it to run DemoShine as a standalone app with no browser chrome, working offline for WebM recordings.

Everything happens locally in your browser. No data is uploaded or stored anywhere.
